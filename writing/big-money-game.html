The Big Money Game

A framework for understanding the games people choose when they want to create significant economic value.

Most career advice starts with a job title, a skill or an industry. I find it more useful to start one level above: what economic game are you actually playing?

The Big Money Game is a working framework for thinking about markets, value creation, distribution, ownership and leverage. It asks what a person sells, to whom, through what vehicle, and where they sit in the value chain.

The questions

  • What market has enough economic value?
  • What painful problem can you solve?
  • Who has the money and authority to buy?
  • How is value delivered?
  • What part of the value can you capture?
  • What leverage can compound over time?

This is a living framework. I expect the model to change as I test it against real businesses and real people.
